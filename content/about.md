## Who I Am

I started writing software in 2018. My first commit was in PHP for my university. Since then, I've worked with several different stacks, but now I spend most of my time in **Go** professionally and **Odin** personally.

## What I Do

Professionally, I build backend systems: distributed services, event pipelines, developer tooling. I'm drawn to problems where understanding the system deeply is non-negotiable before you can build anything that holds up.

Recently, I've been building games. Not in Unity or Godot, but I build them from closer to the ground, in Odin with either Raylib or SDL3. I like knowing how the pieces fit together. I like the constraint of doing more with less.

## What I Value

- **Simplicity over cleverness.** The best code is the code you don't have to write. The second-best is the code that makes sense at 2am.
- **Tools that stay out of your way.** The best tools should be _invisible_.
- **Depth over breadth.** I'd rather understand one thing well than ten things shallowly.
